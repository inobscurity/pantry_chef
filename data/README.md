# Bundled recipe data

`forkrecipe.json` is adapted from the
[ForkRecipe open recipe dataset](https://github.com/futurechef/forkrecipe-recipes) by FoodML and
the ForkRecipe contributors. It is licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), as the original is.

Changes from the original: stocks, condiments, fermented foods, drinks and recipe forks are left
out; each recipe keeps its title, cuisine, category, total time, ingredients with quantities, and
the written instructions for each step. Rebuild it with `scripts/build_forkrecipe.py`.

Pantry Chef isn't affiliated with or endorsed by ForkRecipe or FoodML.
